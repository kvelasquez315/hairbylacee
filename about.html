Lacee's Story

Built on passion, driven by results

Lacee has been in the hair industry for two years — and in that time, she's excelled in every environment she's walked into. She didn't just get good at hair, she became someone her clients genuinely trust. New clients find her through Instagram or word of mouth after seeing her work, and they don't stop coming back.

Her specialty is hair extensions, and she's not just dabbling — she's licensed for it. Whether you want subtle length, serious volume, or a full transformation, Lacee has the training and the eye to make extensions look completely natural. Beyond extensions, she's fully proficient across all hair services: cuts, color, treatments, and everything in between.

The vibe is warm, fun, and real. Lacee loves to chat, she's genuinely invested in getting your hair right, and she brings the kind of energy that makes a salon appointment feel like a good time rather than a chore.
