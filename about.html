How It Started

Built with passion, one client at a time

Lumière was founded in 2012 by head stylist Claire Beaumont, with a simple but powerful belief: every person who walks through our doors deserves to leave feeling more themselves. Not a version of someone else's trend — but genuinely, beautifully them.

What started as a one-chair studio in the heart of the city has grown into a full-service salon with eight talented stylists. We've stayed true to our roots: personal attention, exceptional technique, and a space where people feel genuinely welcomed.

Our name means "light" in French — and that's exactly what we aim to bring out in every client.
